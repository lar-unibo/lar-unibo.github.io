About the lab

From deformable objects to human–robot collaboration

The Laboratory of Automation and Robotics (LAR) is a research laboratory based in Bologna, Italy, part of the Department of Electrical, Electronic, and Information Engineering “Guglielmo Marconi” (DEI) at the Alma Mater Studiorum – Università di Bologna.

Our research follows two main directions. The first is the perception and manipulation of deformable objects, such as cables, wires and wiring harnesses, which robots still struggle to see and handle in industry. The second is shared autonomy and human–robot collaboration: sEMG interfaces, robotic hands and assistive devices that let people and robots work together naturally. Across both, we combine AI-based approaches with classic control theory, so that robot manipulators can operate skillfully in structured and unstructured environments.

All research topics
01 · Main direction

Deformable objects: perception and manipulation

Robots that see and handle cables, wires and wiring harnesses: deep-learning perception, automatic dataset generation, 3D shape estimation, and model- and learning-based manipulation.

Summary of Key Results
02 · Main direction

Shared autonomy, human–robot collaboration and robotic hands

Robots that work with people: sEMG interfaces for robotic hands and assistive devices, shared-autonomy grip regulation, and intuitive kinesthetic teaching.
